# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Monitoring XS harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, and focus technical discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository's security advisory interface. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
